Scenario

A midsized organization surveyed 30 employees as part of an internal study of employee satisfaction and work experiences. The organization wants to better understand its workforce before deciding what questions deserve further investigation.

Your assignment is to explore the dataset and determine what it tells you about the organization's workforce.

Assignment Questions

Open a part to view its questions while you work.

Part 1: Understand the Data

Select four variables. For each, identify its level of measurement, explain whether the mean, median, or mode is appropriate, and identify an appropriate measure of variability. Explain why your choices are appropriate.

Part 2: Describe the Employees

Select three quantitative variables. For each, calculate the mean, median, range, variance, and standard deviation. Then explain which statistic or statistics provide the most useful description of each variable and why.

Part 3: Investigate Something Interesting

Explore the dataset and identify one pattern, difference, unusual result, or observation that deserves further investigation. Provide numerical evidence. You may investigate work arrangement, satisfaction, productivity, salary, absence, hours worked, group differences, or another pattern you discover. You are not expected to establish cause and effect.

Part 4: Statistical Judgment
  1. Based on your analysis, make one recommendation for something the organization should investigate further. Explain what evidence led to your recommendation.
  2. Identify one observation that could potentially have a large influence on a statistical conclusion. Explain what makes it unusual, which statistic or conclusion it could affect, and why its influence should be considered. Do not automatically remove an observation simply because it is unusual.

What to Submit

Submit one document in Canvas containing your answers to Parts 1–4. Include your Case Code and Verification Code. You do not need to submit the CSV unless your instructor asks for it.
